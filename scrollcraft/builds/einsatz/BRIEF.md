# Im Einsatz: brief

Interview answers are in `../../SHARED-BRIEF.md`. Everything below is an **authored decision made within those answers** (the owner chose: different customer per version, mix of boldness, four complete pages, both actions equally important). Nothing here is a user quotation.

## Decisions

1. **Vibe:** Werkstattplakat, Transporterlack, laut. References: van side lettering, hand-pasted event posters, a site foreman's dry humor.
2. **Audience:** regional owner-operators (Autohaus, Fitnessstudio, Praxis, Gewerbe) who judge a firm by whether the crew looks like it really works.
3. **Must believe:** "Die arbeiten wirklich, und zwar die selbst."
4. **Action:** `Analyse-Termin anfragen` and `0971 12382990`, equal weight, always reachable.
5. **Range:** as loud as possible while still selling. Maximalist, one hue family.
6. **Structure:** distinct hard cuts, not one world. Grammar: rhythmic cutlist.
7. **Assets:** real Instagram footage and the real van only. Nothing generated.
8. **Signature seed:** "Die Kolonne arbeitet nur, wenn Sie scrollen."

## Peak

"Ich hab aufgehört zu scrollen, und auf einmal stand die ganze Putzkolonne still, und dann stand da groß: Sie stoppen, wir stoppen." Lives in act 9, held in the fixed chrome layer as the cutlist rule requires. Silence before it: act 8.

**It's the site where** the whole cleaning crew only works while your thumb is moving, and stops dead when you do.

**Authored silence:** act 8 is deliberately near-empty. Not dead scroll.

## Grammar and why the others lost

Rhythmic cutlist. Filmic one-shot needs one held arc, wrong for a crew shown in many jobs. Chaptered editorial and gallery read too composed for "loud". Live surface is the Objekt-Finder's job. Continuous world needs footage this shaky cannot give. Poster bans the footage that is the point. Split stage is a two-sided argument, not this.
