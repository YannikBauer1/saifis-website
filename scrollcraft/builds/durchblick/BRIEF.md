# Durchblick: brief

Authored decisions within the interview answers in `../../SHARED-BRIEF.md` (not a separate interview). Verbatim answers live there.

- **Customer:** businesses and property managers with glass fronts and facades.
- **Boldness:** bold, but serious. A property manager should still trust it and call.
- **Vibe:** clear, physical, bright once clean. Reference: the feeling of a fresh pane after the squeegee passes.
- **Structure:** one unbroken arc, dirty to clear.
- **Assets:** only real Instagram footage. No generated imagery. Procedural dirt film drawn in canvas.
- **Action:** `Analyse-Termin anfragen` and `0971 12382990`, equal weight everywhere.

## Grammar

Filmic one-shot. The other seven lost: chaptered editorial needs long-form substance and bans the scrub hero; live surface is for tools (Objekt-Finder takes it); continuous world would need a one-place flight the footage cannot give; typographic poster would throw away the footage; gallery has no single belief to install; split stage is a two-sided argument (Akte owns before and after); cutlist is the loud version (Einsatz owns it). This page is one change of state, which is what a one-shot does.

## Journey

1. Recognition: the glass is dirty, and it is yours.
2. Turn: your hand cleans it, and the mark left behind is the S.
3. Tension: what bad cleaning actually costs (site's own four complaints).
4. Peak: the water and the sun.
5. Substance: buildings, then method.
6. Range: fourteen services.
7. Commitment: the same window, now clear.

## Feeling curve

1. Unease: a grimy film over everything, the view is there but you cannot have it.
2. Satisfaction: the blade pulls clean glass through the dirt under your own hand, ending on the S. Then relief as you pass through the window into open sky.
3. Recognition, quiet: one plain sentence on white, nothing moves.
4. Awe (peak): water sheets down the pane with the sun coming through it.
5. Evidence: real buildings travel past, labelled plainly.
6. Confidence: four concrete promises, one at a time.
7. Orientation: everything they do, in order of importance.
8. Resolve: the opening window, now clear, two actions, it holds.

## Peak

"Das Wasser lief über die Scheibe, und genau dann kam die Sonne durch." Act: `.peak`. Largest span on the page (4.4). The quiet act before it is the silence.

## Tell someone

"It's the site where you clean the dirty window yourself by scrolling, and you end up having drawn their logo."

## Authored silence

The quiet act has no motion beyond its entrance. Intentional.

## Score

| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Glass | `pin` + bespoke canvas, planes | The hand has to do the cleaning, and the stage must hold still for it |
| Quiet | `flow` | Stillness in front of the peak |
| Peak | `scrub` | The one clip, in the hand, near native size |
| Buildings | `pan` | Breadth reads sideways |
| Method | `pin` with state | Four promises, one at a time |
| Services | `flow` | Reference reads as a document |
| Close | `pin`, held | Resolves and stays |

Five families, no repeats in a row, one scrub, one peak. Total about 19 viewport-heights: longer than the usual reference, because the rail, method and service list carry real content. Nothing is padding.

## Signature move

The S in the glass. Canvas film, destination-out stroke along the logo's S with a wet rim, running water tracks, a blade and pole riding the head, ends cut on the logo's lean.

## Layer contract

| Plane | Asset | Movement | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Far | `plate.webp` sky and facade | Least, slow push | Clean plate, no frame in it |
| Near | `near.webp`, keyed from `748552361_*` | Slight scale, then a push through a real pane | Dark interior opaque, panes alpha, edges decontaminated |
| Film | Procedural canvas | Fixed to the viewer, wiped by scroll | The visitor's own glass, so it sits in front. The brief said mid plane. Moved in front because the beam across the window would have broken the S |
| Type | Semantic HTML | Stable | Sibling scrim only |
