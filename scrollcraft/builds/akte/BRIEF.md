## Grammar

Chaptered editorial. Bans held: no fixed marketing bar, title page without media, hard grounds with no drift, media in captioned columns, no scrub, spotlight, magnet or kinetic.

Why the others lost: filmic hides seams, and this page's honesty depends on showing them. Live surface is too tool-like for proof photos (and is the Objekt-Finder version's job). Continuous world has no geography here. Typographic poster would drop the photos, which are the argument. Gallery forbids persuasion. Split stage would imply a true pair for every case, which the evidence does not support. Cutlist contradicts "calm".

**Allowed exception:** on phones and tablets a small action dock is fixed at the bottom (protocol count and phone number), because the phone number must always be reachable.

**Type choice:** Schibsted Grotesk, a newsroom grotesk, so it reads as reporting rather than advertising. IBM Plex Mono for schema labels and captions only, because forms and file labels are the real tabular use. No serif: nothing about a cleaning company's file justifies one.

## Score

| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | `flow`, type only | The title page is the grammar's hero |
| Unease | `flow` + `in` | Stillness, so the wipe has something to change |
| Relief | `pin` 2.8vh + bespoke wipe | A wipe is a change of state. Largest span on the page |
| Scrutiny | `flow` + `in` | Reading, not watching |
| Calm | `flow`, sticky media column, plain video | The footage carries it |
| Breadth | `flow`, ledger | Same schema makes it a file |
| Confidence | `flow` + `in` stagger | Short reading |
| Resolve | `flow`, working form | The close is an input |

**Deviation:** this uses fewer than four engine device families (flow, pin), plus the bespoke wipe and protocol. I left out `reveal`, `parallax` and `kinetic` on purpose: any second wipe would weaken the one peak on the calmest version.

## Signature move: Das Übergabeprotokoll

Real checkboxes in every chapter write to one protocol that follows the reader (margin on desktop, drawer on phone) and persists for the session. At the close it becomes the inquiry: mailto text, print sheet, next to the phone number. No "sent" state is ever shown. The form says the mail program opens and nothing goes out until the visitor sends it.

## Feel check

Scrolled cold, one word per act: statement, uncomfortable, satisfying, checking, plain, broad, reassuring, practical. Matches the curve, with one difference: act 5 reads plainer than "calm", because the handheld footage is soft. Left as is, because it is honest.
