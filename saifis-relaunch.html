What the winners share

  • Headline = service + city in the first screen
  • Quote form or "Angebot in 24 h" promise above the fold
  • Trust row: reviews, certificates, guild, years
  • One block per service with a target group
  • Real photos of the team and vehicles
  • City-specific pages or sections for the service area

Where nobody in the region plays

  • Motion and brand personality (all sites are static templates)
  • Reviews used as the main argument
  • Transparent explanation of how the price is built
  • Photovoltaik cleaning as a growth niche (rooftop PV is booming in Lower Franconia)
  • A friendly, young team shown as a strength instead of hidden

What all three prototypes fix

  • 01
    First screen sells. Service + city in the eyebrow, a claim in the headline, two calls to action, and the review rating visible before scrolling.
  • 02
    Quote request in 2 minutes. Every variant has a form with service selection and a "Antwort in 24 h" promise, plus a sticky phone/quote bar on mobile.
  • 03
    Reviews as the main proof. 5.0 rating tile, the two real quotes, and a link to the Google profile.
  • 04
    Every service explained. Description, target group, and the keyword in the heading (e.g. "Photovoltaikreinigung"). Ready to expand into subpages later.
  • 05
    Local SEO structure. Service area section listing Bad Kissingen, Bad Bocklet, Münnerstadt, Hammelburg, Bad Brückenau, Oerlenbach, Bad Neustadt, Schweinfurt. Meta description with city and services.
  • 06
    Motion with a reason. Each variant has one orchestrated hero moment and scroll reveals via GSAP; all respect reduced-motion settings.
  • 07
    Mobile first, accessible. Tested at 390px, visible focus states, labels on every input, dark mode support in A and C.

Technical notes

How the prototypes are built

  • Single self-contained HTML file per variant, no build step
  • GSAP 3.12 + ScrollTrigger from cdnjs for motion
  • Google Fonts, everything else inline (CSS, SVG icons, canvas)
  • Standalone versions are in the saifis-website repository

Going live

  • Host on the existing domain; set a 301 redirect from the umlaut domain to saifis-gebaeudereinigung.de
  • Title tag: "Gebäudereinigung Bad Kissingen | Saifi's – Unterhalts-, Glas- & PV-Reinigung"
  • Add LocalBusiness JSON-LD with NAP and opening hours
  • Complete the Google Business profile with the same data and photos
  • Optional later: one subpage per service for SEO depth
